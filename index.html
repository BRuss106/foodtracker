    Likely triggers

    🩺 These are patterns in your log, not a diagnosis. Symptoms happened more often after these ingredients, which doesn't prove they caused them. Bring the list to a doctor or dietitian, who can help confirm it safely (for example with a supervised elimination diet).
    How is this worked out?

    For every meal, the app checks whether the chosen symptom started inside the reaction window after it (for example 2 to 48 hours later). Then for each ingredient it compares two numbers:

    • After meals with it: how often the symptom followed meals containing that ingredient.
    • After meals without it: how often the symptom followed all other meals.

    Times more often divides the first by the second (adjusted so a handful of meals can't give silly numbers). Before episodes counts how many symptom episodes had that ingredient eaten in the window beforehand. The signal label also uses a standard statistics test (Fisher's exact test) to judge whether the difference could easily be luck.

    Meals whose window hasn't finished yet (for example, today's dinner) are left out until it has. Ingredients that are almost always eaten together can't be told apart; they're marked so you can try eating them separately.

    Backup

    ☁️

    Everything is stored only on this phone. Nothing is sent anywhere. If the app is deleted or the phone is lost, the log goes with it, so save a backup every week or so.

    Choose Save to Files and pick iCloud Drive so it's kept off the phone too.

    Restore from a backup

    Use this on a new phone, after reinstalling, or to bring in a backup from the PC version. It replaces what's on this phone.
